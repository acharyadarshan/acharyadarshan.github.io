A bit about me

🇳🇵 I’m from Nepal, a country of mountains, prayer flags, and skies that somehow feel closer. Being surrounded by the Himalayas taught me early on to respect nature and its quiet power.

🥾 Hiking is my happy place. I don’t chase summits just for photos. I hike for the calm, the rhythm of my breath, and the feeling of being small in a beautiful way.

🏡 I feel genuinely blessed when it comes to my family. Their support and presence have shaped who I am more than I sometimes realize.

🌄 Another hill I’d die on. Sunrises in the mountains are better than sunsets anywhere else.

✨ I believe life doesn’t need to be rushed. Growth can be slow, gentle, and still meaningful, just like a long walk uphill.
